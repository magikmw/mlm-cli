## 1. Summary

`status`'s pace hint and `est. EOD` both read off
`gap = required − fulfillment`. Fulfillment counts completed stints
only (§2.4), so an open stint's live minutes never enter `gap`. The
two figures then misbehave while you work:

- `est. EOD = now + gap` moves later minute by minute. You never reach
  it until you `stop`, which turns the open stint into completed time.
- The pace hint's `X left to <required>` stays frozen for the whole
  open stint.

SPEC §2.4 says the open stint feeds "the estimated-EOD figure", but
§7.1 says the opposite ("Fulfillment here still excludes the open
stint's live minutes"), and the code follows §7.1. This changeset
reverses §7.1's exclusion for the pace hint and `est. EOD`.

| Figure | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Pace hint | `gap = required − fulfillment` | `gap = required − fulfillment − live` |
| `est. EOD` | `now + (required − fulfillment)` | `open start + (required − fulfillment)` |

For a start at or before now, `open start = now − live`, so `est. EOD`
equals `now + gap`. Punches are minute-aligned and `HH:MM` truncates
seconds, so the printed `est. EOD` is the same on every read while the
stint runs. It moves only when a punch is added or removed.

## 1.1 Non-goals

- No change to the day total. It still sums completed stints only and
  keeps its `(+ ongoing)` flag.
- No change to the week line, its fulfillment parenthetical, `owed`,
  carry-in, or anything in `mlm week`. Live minutes still never enter
  a stored or carried figure (§2.4).
- No change to `required`, the daily target, or the weekday-cap
  wording (`required today` on Fri–Sun).
- No string changes in the pace hint or `est. EOD`. Which string
  prints at a given moment does change (§1.2).
- No change to past-date `status`. It shows neither figure.
- No change to pairing (§4.3) or the splice (§4.3.1).
- No live credit for an open start dated yesterday (§1.2).

## 1.2 Possible issues left after this changeset

- **Pace hint vs day total.** The pace hint now counts the running
  stint, but the day total next to it does not.
- **Pace hint vs week line.** The pace hint counts down while the week
  line's `owed` stays frozen until `stop`. On Fri–Sun, where `required`
  equals the week target, the two can disagree in direction, e.g.
  `00h 10m over 40h 00m required today` beside
  `Week …: 00h 20m left by end of Friday`. The gap also shows in
  magnitude, e.g. README's Saturday sample reads `09h 54m over` beside
  `04h 35m ahead of target`. `mlm week` for the current week likewise
  stays frozen while `status` counts live.
- **Output changes minute by minute.** Two `status` runs with no punch
  in between print different pace-hint figures. When the running stint
  closes the gap, the hint flips from `left to` to `over` and
  `est. EOD HH:MM` becomes `target already met` mid-stint.
- **Jumps move to backdated punches.** `stop` no longer makes the hint
  jump, because live minutes already counted. A backdated `stop HH:MM`
  raises the hint by the minutes it cuts off. A backdated
  `start HH:MM` lowers it by the minutes it adds.
- **`est. EOD` means something new.** It used to mean "if you keep
  going from right now". It now means "when this open stint closes the
  gap". Once a stint runs past it, `status` prints `target already met`
  and no clock time. A break (`stop`, then `start`) moves it later by
  the break's length.
- **Day suffix appears early.** `est. EOD` is fixed per stint, so its
  day suffix (§3.4) shows from the first `status` after the `start` and
  never flips while working.
- **Start typed for later today.** `est. EOD` projects from that start
  (§3.3), so it sits more than the hint's `left to` figure ahead of the
  clock, while the hint does not count down.
- **Two or more open starts.** The stint list shows every open stint
  with its own duration, but the hint and `est. EOD` credit only the
  most recent one (§3.2). Fixing the E7 anomaly makes both jump.
- **Overnight session after midnight.** A stint started yesterday and
  still open after midnight is not on today's page, so it gets no live
  credit and no `est. EOD`. At 00:00 the hint jumps, because `required`
  steps up a weekday while `live` drops to 0. Same root as the existing
  §1.2a issue on yesterday's open session being invisible today.

## 2. Terms

- **live**: `ctx.day.open.last().minutes_so_far` in `status::resolve`
  — the most recent open start dated today, whole minutes from its
  `start` to now, clamped at 0 (§4.3). It is 0 when `ctx.day.open` is
  empty. `ctx.day` is the source, not `ctx.day_raw`, because a splice
  onto tomorrow (§4.3.1) can consume today's raw open start.
- **open start**: the same `ctx.day.open.last()` start's instant.

## 3. Behavior

### 3.1 `gap` subtracts live minutes

On today's `status`, `gap = required − fulfillment − live`.
`DailyTargetHint.gap_minutes` holds this post-live value. Every
existing rule keyed on `gap` keeps its threshold:

- Pace hint reads `X left to <required>` when `gap ≥ 0`, and
  `X over <required>` when `gap < 0`, with `X = |gap|`.
- With an open stint and `gap > 0`, `est. EOD` prints
  `open start + (required − fulfillment)`.
- With an open stint and `gap ≤ 0`, `target already met` prints.
- `est. EOD` gains ` (tomorrow)` when it lands after today's midnight,
  as before.
- Without an open stint, `live = 0`. The pace hint matches baseline
  output exactly, and `est. EOD` stays omitted.

Worked example, matching §7.1's sample at 18:00 on Thursday: completed
time `07h 25m`, open stint `17:45-now` (`live = 15`), `required −
fulfillment = 02h 45m`.

```
Before: Day total:     07h 25m (+ ongoing), 02h 45m left to 32h 00m required by end of Thursday, est. EOD 20:45
After:  Day total:     07h 25m (+ ongoing), 02h 30m left to 32h 00m required by end of Thursday, est. EOD 20:30
```

At 18:30 the same day the after line reads `02h 00m left`, still
`est. EOD 20:30`.

### 3.2 Which open stint counts

`ctx.day` pairs only punches dated today. An open start from an
earlier date never appears in `ctx.day.open`:

- Yesterday's open start sits on yesterday's page and shows nothing on
  today's (§1.2).
- A start 2–14 days old shows only as a lookback `[!]` line.
- A start 15 or more days old shows nothing on today's page.

So `live` never spans midnight or a week boundary, and a stale
`(+ unclosed)` stint never feeds `gap`.

When today has two or more open starts (E7, §7.3), `live` uses the
most recent one, which is `ctx.day.open.last()` because `open` is
sorted by start ascending. Summing them would count the same
wall-clock minutes twice. The most recent start gives the smallest
`live`, so the hint and `est. EOD` err late rather than early.

### 3.3 Open start later than now

A `start` with a TIME later than now is legal (SPEC §3.2). Its `live` is
clamped to 0, so the pace hint matches baseline. `est. EOD` projects
from the start's own time, `open start + (required − fulfillment)`,
which is when the work would end if it begins as typed.

### 3.4 Day suffix on `est. EOD`

`est. EOD` names the day it lands on whenever that is not today:

| Lands | Suffix | Example |
|---|---|---|
| today | none | `est. EOD 17:30` |
| 1 day later | ` (tomorrow)` | `est. EOD 01:15 (tomorrow)` |
| 2–6 days later | ` (<weekday abbrev>)` | `est. EOD 06:20 (Mon)` |
| 7+ days later | ` (<weekday abbrev> <YYYY-MM-DD>)` | `est. EOD 06:20 (Mon 2026-10-12)` |

The weekday abbreviation matches the `status` header's. This replaces
SPEC §7.1's rule that the suffix is always the bare word `(tomorrow)`.

## 5. Test plan

Behavior tests run through `status::resolve` with a fixed `now` and a
real connection. Hand-built `StatusView` fixtures (the §7.1 sample at
status.rs:1102 and status.rs:1391) only check layout, and their
figures are updated to the "after" line for consistency.

- T1: open stint, `gap > 0` after subtracting live. Pace hint and
  `est. EOD` both use the reduced `gap`.
- T2: stability. Same punches, two `now`s 30 minutes apart, both with
  non-zero seconds. Same `est. EOD`; pace hint drops by 30 minutes.
- T3: live tips `gap` from positive to negative. Pace hint flips to
  `over`, `est. EOD` becomes `target already met`.
- T4: live makes `gap` exactly 0. Pace hint reads `00h 00m left to`,
  `est. EOD` reads `target already met`.
- T5: no open stint. Pace hint and `gap_minutes` equal
  `required − fulfillment`. The existing resolve-level assertions on
  `gap_minutes` (status.rs:2005, status.rs:2105) are re-checked
  against the post-live meaning.
- T6: two open starts today at different times. `live` equals the
  later start's minutes.
- T7: `(tomorrow)` suffix still fires when `est. EOD` crosses midnight.
- T8: yesterday-dated open start, today has no punches. `live = 0`, no
  `est. EOD`, hint matches baseline.
- T9: open start later than now. Hint matches baseline, `est. EOD`
  equals that start plus `required − fulfillment`.
- T10: Friday with an open stint. `required today` wording, `gap`
  includes live.
