# Live open stint in pace hint and est. EOD — design spec

**Baseline**: written against `main` at `c9e980b` (`v0.3.6` plus the
unreleased `open-punch-visibility` changeset).
**Status**: draft, awaiting user review.

## 1. Summary

`status`'s pace hint and `est. EOD` both read off
`gap = required − fulfillment`. Fulfillment counts completed stints
only (§2.4), so an open stint's live minutes never enter `gap`. The
two figures then misbehave while you work:

- `est. EOD = now + gap` moves later minute by minute. You never reach
  it until you `stop`, which turns the open stint into completed time.
- The pace hint's `X left to <required>` stays frozen for the whole
  open stint.

SPEC §2.4 already says the open stint feeds "the estimated-EOD
figure". §7.1's formula never did. This changeset makes §7.1 match.

The fix subtracts the open stint's live minutes from `gap` for both
figures:

| Figure | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Pace hint | `gap = required − fulfillment` | `gap = required − fulfillment − live` |
| `est. EOD` | `now + (required − fulfillment)` | `now + (required − fulfillment − live)` |

`now − live` is the open stint's start instant, so `est. EOD` equals
`open start + (required − fulfillment)`. It stays fixed while the
stint runs. It moves only when a punch is added or removed. Whole-minute
rounding of `live` may shift it by one minute between reads.

## 1.1 Non-goals

- No change to the day total. It still sums completed stints only and
  keeps its `(+ ongoing)` flag.
- No change to the week line, its fulfillment parenthetical, `owed`,
  carry-in, or anything in `mlm week`. Live minutes still never enter
  a stored or carried figure (§2.4).
- No change to `required`, the daily target, or the weekday-cap
  wording (`required today` on Fri–Sun).
- No change to the pace hint's or `est. EOD`'s wording. Only the
  numbers change.
- No change to past-date `status`. It shows neither figure.
- No change to pairing (§4.3) or the splice (§4.3.1).

## 1.2 Known issues left after this changeset

- The pace hint now changes while you read it, but the day total next
  to it does not. That split is deliberate. The day total reports
  today in isolation, and the pace hint reports how much is left.

## 2. Terms

- **live**: the open stint's `minutes_so_far` (§4.3, whole minutes
  from its `start` to now, clamped at 0). It is 0 when today has no
  open stint.
- **open stint on today's page**: an unmatched `start` dated today.
  Today's `status` already shows only these (§3.2).

## 3. Behavior

### 3.1 `gap` subtracts live minutes

On today's `status`, `gap = required − fulfillment − live`. The pace
hint and `est. EOD` both use this `gap`. Every existing rule keyed on
`gap` keeps its threshold:

- Pace hint reads `X left to <required>` when `gap ≥ 0`, and
  `X over <required>` when `gap < 0`, with `X = |gap|`.
- `est. EOD HH:MM` prints `now + gap` when `gap > 0`.
- `target already met` prints when `gap ≤ 0` and an open stint exists.
- `est. EOD` gains ` (tomorrow)` when `now + gap` lands after
  midnight, as before.
- Without an open stint, `live = 0`. The pace hint matches today's
  output exactly, and `est. EOD` stays omitted.

Worked example, matching §7.1's sample at 18:00 on Thursday: completed
time `07h 25m`, open stint `17:45-now` (`live = 15`), `required −
fulfillment = 02h 45m`.

```
Before: Day total:     07h 25m (+ ongoing), 02h 45m left to 32h 00m required by end of Thursday, est. EOD 20:45
After:  Day total:     07h 25m (+ ongoing), 02h 30m left to 32h 00m required by end of Thursday, est. EOD 20:30
```

At 18:30 the same day the after line reads `02h 00m left`, still
`est. EOD 20:30`.

### 3.2 Which open stint counts

Today's page only holds open starts dated today. A start from an
earlier date never appears there as open. A forgotten one shows up
only as a lookback `[!]` line, and an overnight one sits on
yesterday's page. So `live` never spans midnight or a week boundary,
and a stale `(+ unclosed)` stint never feeds `gap`.

When today has two or more open starts (an E7 anomaly, §7.3), `live`
is the **most recent** one's `minutes_so_far`. Summing them would
count the same wall-clock minutes twice. Taking the most recent gives
the smallest `live`, so `est. EOD` errs late rather than early. The
`[!]` line already flags the extra start.

## 5. Test plan

In `status.rs` tests, against `resolve` with a fixed `now`:

- T1: open stint, `gap > 0` after subtracting live. Pace hint and
  `est. EOD` both use the reduced `gap`.
- T2: stability. Same punches, `now` 30 minutes apart, same
  `est. EOD`; pace hint drops by 30 minutes.
- T3: live tips `gap` from positive to negative. Pace hint flips to
  `over`, `est. EOD` becomes `target already met`.
- T4: live makes `gap` exactly 0. Pace hint reads `00h 00m left to`,
  `est. EOD` reads `target already met`.
- T5: no open stint. Output byte-identical to baseline.
- T6: two open starts today. `live` uses the later one.
- T7: `(tomorrow)` suffix still fires when `now + gap` crosses
  midnight.
- Update the existing §7.1 sample assertions to the "after" line.
